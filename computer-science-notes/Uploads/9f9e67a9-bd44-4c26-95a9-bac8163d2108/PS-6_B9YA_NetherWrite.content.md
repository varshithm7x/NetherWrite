0 1 · T H E TA R G E T 
PROBLEM STATEMENT & UNDERSTANDING 
PROBLEM STATEMENT 
• Traditional learning follows a one-size-fits-all
approach.
• Students struggle to identify knowledge gaps and
retain concepts.
• Learners with limited connectivity lack reliable AI
assistance. 
OUR UNDERSTANDING 
• Learning materials are fragmented across notes, PDFs,
and resources.
• Most AI tutors lack persistent understanding of
individual progress.
• Cloud-dependent solutions raise privacy, cost, and
accessibility concerns.

## Page 3

0 2 · T H E P L A N 
PROPOSED SOLUTION 
OUR IDEA 
• A local-first AI learning workspace built around
students' own knowledge.
• Connects notes and concepts using semantic
understanding and knowledge graphs.
• Proposes personalized explanations, quizzes, and
study recommendations.
• Designed for privacy, offline access, and efficient local
processing.
